🏥 Operational Realities & Legal Escape Hatches

While the financial risks have been capped, specific operational mandates remain in the text of the lease. The Landlord has provided contractual mechanisms to deal with these without altering the master Condominium Declaration.

⛔ The Catholic Directives

The Declaration technically mandates compliance with Catholic ethical teachings, restricting standard GYN practices.

The v2 Solution: "Don't Ask, Don't Tell." If the Condo Board ever enforces this and fines the Landlord, you must indemnify the fine, but you have the immediate contractual right to terminate the lease and walk away.

🔧 The HVAC Amortization

Instead of full replacement liability for an aging system left by a previous tenant.

The v2 Solution: For any HVAC repair over $750 or a full replacement, your financial responsibility is strictly limited to the amortized fraction equal only to your remaining months on the lease.
